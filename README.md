* **Title:** Towards a Geometric Interpretation of Electron Spin: A Research Programme on 360° Periodicity, Internal Topology, and Superconducting Pairing
* **Abstract:** We propose a geometric research programme re-examining the carrier of the electron's rotational phase. We conjecture that the electron possesses an intrinsic internal geometry for which a 360° rotation constitutes a complete topological event, with conventional 720° periodicity reflecting global spinor representation topology. Furthermore, this internal geometry provides a geometric descriptor for Cooper pair topology in superconductors, complementing the BCS mechanism.

* **The Hidden Thread of Structural Information Evolution (HTSIE)**
  * *Abstract:* Proposes a unified framework linking condensed matter, quantum plasma, and black holes. Shows that energy storage migrates from particle kinetics to boundary encoding, unifying the Chandrasekhar, TOV, and Bekenstein limits as expressions of a single information principle.
* **Invariant Light-Cone Algebra and Emergent Causal Structure**
  * *Abstract:* Formulates a pre-geometric algebra where light speed invariance emerges from a primitive $C^*$-algebraic structure rather than a spacetime postulate.
* **Fractal Folding Theory: Kakeya Conjecture to Quantum Gravity**
  * *Abstract:* Links the Wang-Zahl (2025) 3D Kakeya set proof to Planck-scale spacetime folding dynamics. Computes spectral dimension scaling via RBN simulations and provides observable predictions for black hole echo delays ($\tau_0 \approx 355\ \mu\text{s}$).
* **Time as Irreversible Spectral-Dimension Flow with a 1/3 Power Law**
  * *Abstract:* Proposes time as an emergent property of spatial spectral dimension flow ($D_s: 3 \to 2$). Replaces the Big Bang singularity with a smooth cosmological bounce and yields falsifiable predictions for sub-millimeter fifth-force experiments.
* **Spectral Dimension Flow & Black Hole Horizon Defects**
  * *Abstract:* Models the black hole horizon as a localized non-orientable Möbius-type defect under metric scale transformations, deriving covariant emergent time via causal cone collapse.
</details>
